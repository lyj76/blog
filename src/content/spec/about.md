## 研究兴趣

我的主要研究与兴趣交汇于现代数学与人工智能之间：

::::note
**现代数学基础** —— 我目前的学习重心正处于现代数学的基础阶段，主要集中在点集拓扑与实分析。这是通往更深层抽象结构的必经之路，我希望借此尽快过渡到基于测度论的现代概率论体系，为后续研究高维空间与统计学习提供严密的推演工具。
::::

::::tip

::::

# English

> *Sample the empirical, extract the non-trivial, formalize the truth.*

Hi, I'm **QuamtumDusk**, currently a master's student in Artificial Intelligence.

When it comes to intelligence and the unknown, I believe empirical observation is only the starting point. What matters is distilling non-trivial patterns from experience and pinning them down in rigorous, formal language. This quote is not just my motto; it is the core principle behind everything I write and think about here.

## Research Interests

My research and interests sit at the intersection of modern mathematics and AI:

::::note
**Foundations of Modern Mathematics** —— I am currently working through the fundamentals of modern mathematics, focusing on point-set topology and real analysis. This is the necessary path toward deeper abstract structures; I hope to move quickly into measure-theoretic probability, which will give me rigorous tools for studying high-dimensional spaces and statistical learning.
::::

::::tip
**AI and Large Language Models** —— My focus goes beyond surface-level phenomena and applications. I care about the underlying mechanisms behind emergent intelligence, how information is represented inside models, and the fine-grained training details across the pipeline from pretraining to alignment (SFT, RLHF, and beyond). Finding the theoretical grounding behind these complex empirical phenomena is what keeps me exploring.
::::

## This Blog

- **Research & Experiments**: daily research progress, hands-on projects, reproductions of frontier papers, and experimental data analysis.
- **Theory & Derivation**: systematic notes on modern mathematics (topology, real analysis, etc.) and mathematical derivations behind AI algorithms.
- **Reflections & Synthesis**: narrating and restructuring the essence of knowledge while reading, coding, and experimenting.

Feel free to reach out if you share an interest in these fields.
